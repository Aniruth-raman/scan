DocAligner document-corner model
=================================

This app bundles the DocAligner corner-detection model.

  Model:  lcnet100_h_e_bifpn_256_fp32.onnx
  Source: https://github.com/DocsaidLab/DocAligner  (release 1.1.1)
  Author: DocsaidLab (Ze Yuan)
  Licence: Apache License, Version 2.0
           http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

The ONNX graph was converted to TFLite with onnx2tf for this app; the only
graph change was lowering the BiFPN weighted-fusion Einsum (`i,i...->...`)
into Reshape/Mul/ReduceSum, which is numerically identical. The licence above
applies to the model weights.
